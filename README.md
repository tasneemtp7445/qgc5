*Indiflo Private Limited is a drone manufacturing and aerial technology company incorporated on July 12, 2024. Headquartered in Patna, Bihar (at Chitrakut Nagar, Danapur-Digha), the startup focuses on building indigenous UAV platforms, embedded electronics, and AI-driven autonomous drone operations.*.

---

### 🌟 *Why Choose IndiFloGroundControl?*

- *🚀 Modern Mission Planning
- *✈️ PX4 Compatible.
- *🛠️Custom Drone Operations.

🔍 For a deeper dive into using IGC, check out the [User Manual](https://docs.indiflogroundcontrol.com/en/) – although, thanks to IGC's intuitive UI, you may not even need it!


---

### 🚁 *Key Features*

- 🕹️ *Full Flight Control*: Supports all *MAVLink drones*.
- ⚙️ *Vehicle Setup*: Tailored configuration for *PX4* platforms.
- 🔧 *Fully Open Source*: Customize and extend the software to suit your needs.

🎯 Check out the latest updates in our [New Features and Release Notes](https://github.com/mavlink/indiflogroundcontrol/blob/master/ChangeLog.md).

### 💻 *Get Involved!*

IndiFlo Ground Control is actively developed to support industrial UAV operations. Developers are welcome to extend the platform with custom MAVLink functionality, payload support, camera integration, AI modules, and enterprise drone workflows.
